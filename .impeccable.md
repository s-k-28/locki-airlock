## Design Context

Derived from the project brief written by the team (Dhruv Jain, Mustafa), HackUTD 2026: Zero Day.

### Users
- **Operators** (the two builders) running a live 90-second demo at a crowded hackathon table,
  on a 13-15" laptop, often at night, with judges watching over their shoulder. They need to
  arm, stop and switch modes instantly and never doubt what is real versus simulated.
- **Judges** glancing at the screen for a few seconds. They must immediately see a closed
  physical feedback loop: hand detected -> decision -> safety check -> robot moved.
- **The student** doing a focus session: needs a big, calm timer and should otherwise be
  left alone.

### Brand Personality
Mischievous, vigilant, precise. A cheeky little robot guard with a mission-control panel.
Funny in what it *says*; exact in what it *shows*. Never pretends.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Theme: dark (dim venue, night sessions, projector/laptop demo) - requested by the team:
  near-black, electric cyan, small magenta accents, "Zero Day" cyberpunk.
- Discipline over glow: colour is semantic, not decoration.
  - cyan = live / active signal (camera live, timer running, link healthy)
  - magenta = an intervention happened (phone reach, retreat) - the mischief colour
  - amber = simulated or caution (SIMULATION badges, armed state, degraded link)
  - red = emergency stop only
- Instrument-panel structure: hairline rules, tabular numerals, labelled readouts.
  No glassmorphism, no gradient text, no glow halos, no side-stripe cards, no card-in-card.
- Type: Chakra Petch (display, wordmark, big timer labels), Archivo variable with width axis
  (UI/body, condensed for dense labels), Martian Mono (numbers, frames on the wire, event data).
- Anti-references: generic SaaS admin template, neon "hacker" kitsch, fake HUD noise.

### Design Principles
1. Visual priority is fixed: timer > live camera + robot > event timeline > analytics.
2. Every number has a source; every panel says LIVE, SIMULATED or UNAVAILABLE.
3. Safety controls are one click and always visible (E-STOP never scrolls away).
4. The intervention moment is the hero animation: one magenta pulse that traces the loop.
5. Quiet when focused: nothing moves on screen unless state changed.
